# Code of Conduct: The Root-Care Stewardship Agreement

## Our Pledge
As stewards, creators, and contributors to the Root-Care Ecosystem, we pledge to make participation in our project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone—regardless of experience, physical capability, background, or identity.

## Our Standards
We look to foster a collaborative community focused on open material science, mutual aid, and accessible hygiene. 

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:**
*   Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
*   Offering constructive feedback on recipe variations or craft modifications.
*   Prioritizing user safety, accessibility, and the open-source integrity of the ecosystem.
*   Respecting the absolute privacy and anonymity of all community members.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior include:**
*   Aggressive, insulting, or derogatory language and personal attacks.
*   Attempting to uncover or publish the real-world identities (doxxing) of any contributor.
*   Promoting commercial exploitation, private patenting, or the addition of prohibited chemical stimulants.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior, including the immediate removal of hostile or non-compliant comments and contributions.
