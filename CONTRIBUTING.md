# Contributing to the Root-Care Ecosystem

Thank you for stepping forward to help expand, test, and refine the Root-Care Ecosystem! This project exists as a permanent public utility for accessible hydration, oral care, dermal hygiene, and ocular comfort. Because this repository is a collaborative blueprint, your hands-on research, material testing, and recipe variations are vital to bringing these designs to life.

---

## Contribution Guidelines & Privacy Policy

To protect the safety, privacy, and open-source integrity of this repository, all contributors must strictly adhere to the following framework:

### 1. Absolute Privacy & Anonymity
*   **No Real Names:** To prevent identity scraping, corporate tracking, and privacy vulnerabilities, you are **strictly prohibited** from entering your legal name anywhere in this repository.
*   **Username Safety Filtering:** If your existing digital account handle or platform username contains your real first name, last name, or any identifiable personal nickname, **do not use it to contribute here.** You must either use an entirely randomized technical handle or sign your work using a completely fabricated, non-identifiable creative pen name.
*   **Identification:** All contributions, logged adjustments, and registry entries must be signed exclusively with a digital username or a chosen creative pen name that completely hides your physical identity (e.g., Project Founder updates are signed under the permanent pen name *Mary Florence Sterling* to protect the founder's personal accounts).

### 2. Formulating & Material Restrictions
*   **The Stimulant Ban:** Any recipe variation or additive contribution that introduces isolated caffeine, synthetic stimulants, or high-dose energy compounds will be immediately rejected and deleted. All formulations must remain restorative and deeply hydrating.
*   **The Non-Plastic Standard:** Modifications to the Dermal Dew physical structures must prioritize natural, biodegradable, and sustainable materials (such as local hardwoods, plant fibers, and organic textiles). Avoid synthetic, non-recyclable polymers.
*   **The Ocular Dew Protocol:** Because ocular hygiene handles highly sensitive membranes navigating dry eye and allergy distress, any proposed ophthalmic solutions or compresses must strictly prioritize absolute isotonic balance, complete sterility, and zero volatile aromatic irritants (such as raw essential oils or non-filtered particulates).

---

## How to Contribute

1.  **Formulation Testing:** Mix the Oral Dew syrup bases or emulsion matrices. Log your specific blending observations, viscosity adjustments under different ambient temperatures, or successful local herbal glycerite extractions.
2.  **Textile & Craft Fabrication:** Build the Dermal Dew articulated handle or sew the hourglass fabric sleeves (Cloud, Leaf, Bark, Thorn). Share refinements regarding seam durability, wood sealing, or thread tensile strength.
3.  **Ocular Alignment Research:** Contribute non-irritating, soothing botanical compress methods, sterile saline-hydration ratios, or temperature-retaining eye pad fabric patterns designed specifically to relieve severe dry eye and airborne allergy inflammation.
4.  **Propose a Variation:** If you have adapted the Core or Active lines to include local, regional botanicals from your homestead or urban kitchen space, submit your recipe using clean Cooklang formatting.
