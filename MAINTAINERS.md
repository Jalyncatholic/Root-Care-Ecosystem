# Repository Maintainers: Root-Care Stewardship Team

The Root-Care Ecosystem is an open-source project designed by its founder and maintained by a distributed team of community stewards. Because the project founder focuses exclusively on core conceptual design, material architecture, and formulation research, this file establishes the active maintenance team responsible for daily operations, community outreach, and repository management.

---

## The Operations Framework

To preserve the peace, focus, and security of the design space, a strict separation of labor is maintained:

1.  **The Design Desk:** Handled exclusively by the founder, *Mary Florence Sterling*. All core architectural changes, official line extensions (Oral, Dermal, Ocular), and foundational philosophy updates originate here. The founder does not monitor public forums, issue trackers, or external social media channels.
2.  **The Stewardship Team:** Handled by the active maintainers listed below. The maintainers possess administrative permissions to review pull requests, manage issue trackers, maintain community standards, and handle public engagement across external open-source networks.
