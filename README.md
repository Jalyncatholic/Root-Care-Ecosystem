# The Root-Care Ecosystem

## The Oral Dew Line

### Background & Mission Statement
Modern oral care and hydration delivery systems are heavily industrialized, frequently relying on synthetic texturizers, harsh chemical detergents, and high-dose, dehydrating stimulants. The Root-Care system shifts this paradigm, offering an open-source, maker-accessible framework focused on restorative mucosal hydration, oral care accessibility, and precise cannabinoid delivery. 

This architecture is specifically designed for individuals navigating chronic mucosal dryness, fine motor or hand-eye coordination limitations, or fluid-retention challenges linked to antidiuretic therapies. By utilizing natural plant resins, flexible fibers, and clean glycerin-extracted botanicals, Root-Care transforms routine mechanical oral hygiene into a comforting, automated, and accessible ritual. Grounded in the principles of domestic stewardship and open-source material sciences, this repository provides makers with a completely stimulant-free, adaptable blueprint to clean, protect, and hydrate the oral cavity.

### The Core Hydration Base (The Syrup Matrix)
The Core serves as the foundational, non-reactive fluid vehicle for the entire product ecosystem, optimized for deep mucosal moisture, enamel protection, and clean flavor suspension.

Preheat @vegetable glycerin to 80°C. Add @erythritol and the prepared mineral salt matrix directly to the hot glycerin. Stir continuously until completely dissolved and crystal clear. Infuse desired selection from the aromatic families and pulverized fruit families directly into the heated syrup. Strain out any spent aromatic plant matter through a #100-mesh fine sieve to leave a smooth, flavorful hydration fluid.

### The Active Base (The Cannabinoid Carrier)
The Active base elevates the Core hydration vehicle by integrating target cannabinoids for localized buccal and sublingual absorption.

Blend @extra virgin olive oil and @organic MCT oil together at a 1:1 ratio. Heat gently to 60°C. Dissolve the target cannabis @actives (such as THC or THCv distillates/isolates) completely into the warm lipid phase. Add liquid @sunflower lecithin to the warm oil blend to anchor the emulsification. Slowly stream the lipid blend into the warm water-soluble Core Hydration Base while agitation occurs, creating a permanent, non-separating infusion.

### Flavor & Fruit Family Registries
All flavor elements are handled via direct extraction or micro-suspension to maintain a silky, non-gritty final texture in the oral cavity.

#### Confectionery Aromatic Families (Glycerite Infusions)
Rather than stirring in raw powders, these whole, cracked, or bruised dry botanicals are steeped directly into hot vegetable glycerin to extract their aromatic compounds. The spent plant matter is then strained out completely.
*   Hearthside Family: Whole or cracked @cinnamon bark, bruised @ginger root, whole @cloves, crushed @cardamom pods, grated @nutmeg, whole @star anise, cracked dried @chilies, whole @allspice berries, whole @mace blades, and toasted, cracked @coriander seeds.
*   Crisp Morning Family: Dried @licorice root chips, crushed @fennel seeds, dried @eucalyptus leaves, whole dried @spearmint leaves, whole dried @peppermint leaves, whole @anise seeds, whole @wintergreen leaves, and cut @lemongrass stalks.
*   Cloister Garden Family: Dried @chamomile flowers, whole @lemon balm leaves, @orange blossom petals, whole dried @rose buds, @lavender flowers, @linden blossoms, dried @hibiscus calyces, and dried @elderflowers or @passionflower leaves.
*   Roasted Harvest Family: Whole roasted @cacao nibs (releasing only native, minute trace theobromine), whole split @vanilla beans, @fenugreek seeds, roasted @carob pods, roasted @chicory root pieces, toasted @malt barley kernels, toasted @malt wheat berries, cracked toasted @caraway seeds, and toasted @mesquite pod pieces.

#### Freeze-Dried Fruit Families (Pulverized Micro-Suspensions)
To preserve the vivid, clean flavor profiles of fruits without introducing water or unstrainable, gritty fiber masses, these fruits are freeze-dried and pulverized into an ultra-fine, microscopic powder. They are stirred directly into the liquid base, dissolving and suspending seamlessly within the dense glycerin matrix.
*   Horizon Grove Family: High-acid, saliva-stimulating profiles including @orange zest, @lemon zest, @lime zest, and @grapefruit zest, alongside @mango, @guava, and @pineapple.
*   Sunken Orchard Family: Bright, delicate summer profiles including @strawberry, @raspberry, @peaches, @plums, and bright orchard fruits.
*   The Abundant Hearth Family: Rich, grounding, comforting profiles including sweeter, darker tree fruits like baked @apples or @pears and sweet, starchy culinary squashes like @pumpkin.
*   The Deep Meadow Family: Heavy, intensely sweet, dark profiles including @blackberry, @blueberry, @black cherry, @dates, and roasted @figs.

### Mineral Salt & Electrolyte Matrix (Per 15 mL Serving)
This matrix avoids the harsh, briny taste of standard table salt (sodium chloride), delivering a gentle, functional micro-dose of minerals optimized to buffer acidity, soothe nerves, trigger saliva production, and actively remineralize tooth enamel on contact.
*   Add @sodium citrate{50%mg} to 100 mg as a salivary gland stimulant to raise oral pH and buffer plaque acids.
*   Add @potassium citrate{40%mg} to 80 mg to desensitize exposed dentin nerve tubules and neutralize localized mouth acids.
*   Add @magnesium malate{30%mg} to 60 mg to provide a structural matrix for enamel stability and ease local jaw tension.
*   Add @calcium glycerophosphate{75%mg} to 150 mg as a direct bioavailable enamel remineralization anchor that cross-links with Low-Methoxyl Pectin to stabilize hydrogels.

### The Product Formats
The framework spans three mechanical delivery lines, each split into a Core hydration variant and an Active cannabinoid variant.

#### 1. The Malts
An ultra-smooth hydrogel wrapped completely within an outer layer of toasted semolina malt paper. The outer malt wrapper is completely versatile and zero-waste: it can be safely swallowed as an edible confectionery snack, composted after use, or dropped directly into liquid. 

The Malts feature three distinct operational pathways depending on user needs:
1. The Buccal Pouch Pathway: Slipped comfortably into the cheek pouch to slowly melt away, continuously coating dry oral tissue and releasing its mineral matrix over an extended period.
2. The Beverage Flavoring Pathway: Dropped straight into hot water to dissolve completely, transforming the malt wrap and hydrogel into a clean, mineral-enriched comforting drink. Note: Only Core Malts should be used for the beverage pathway.
3. The Confectionery Snack Pathway: Chewed up directly and swallowed as a quick, functional, and deeply hydrating treat.

Take {15%mL} of either the warm @Core Hydration Base or @Active Base. Add Low-Methoxyl @pectin, @arrowroot, @tapioca starch, and @gum arabic directly into the liquid. Whisk thoroughly using a #whisk until the gelling agents are completely hydrated and smooth. Pour the structured gel into small, bite-sized confectionery #molds to set. Wrap the set hydrogel pieces completely in sheets of toasted @semolina malt paper to finish.

*   Root-Care Core Malts: Crafted using the Core Hydration Base recipe with gelling modifiers. Suitable for all three pathways (buccal pouch, beverage flavoring, or snack).
*   Root-Care Active Malts: Crafted by incorporating the Active Base recipe with gelling modifiers. Restricted exclusively to the buccal pouch and confectionery snack pathways to ensure controlled cannabinoid delivery.

#### 2. The Chews
Hand-cut sections of flexible, non-splintering young bamboo measuring 2 to 4 mm in diameter and 24 to 32 mm in length. Chewing on the tip frays the bamboo fibers into a soft, functional brush that sweeps away food and releases enamel-protecting minerals. When the tip is thoroughly spent, it is cleanly snipped off with household scissors to reset the stick.

Thin out {15%mL} of the chosen flavor-infused @Core or @Active syrup base by adding a small excess of warm @vegetable glycerin to lower its viscosity. Place the dry, hand-cut young @bamboo sections into a #vacuum chamber vessel and submerge them fully in the thinned syrup. Draw a deep vacuum to pull all residual air out of the wood’s native grain. Release the vacuum to force the warm flavor, mineral, and active syrup deep into the microscopic cellular pores of the cane fibers.

*   Root-Care Core Chews: Young bamboo sections deeply infused with the thinned Core Hydration Base.
*   Root-Care Active Chews: Young bamboo sections deeply infused with the thinned Active Base for localized cannabinoid release alongside mechanical cleaning.

#### 3. The Gums
A premium, natural chewing gum cube combining elastic chicle with antimicrobial spruce or mastic resins. Hidden entirely within the lipophilic rubber matrix is a compressed network of high wet-tensile strength plant micro-threads that handle the flossing completely behind the scenes through simple chewing.

Soak loose, ultra-fine micro-threads of high wet-tensile strength @ramie threads, @flax threads, or @hemp threads in the pure @Core flavor syrup until fully saturated with hydration and taste. In a separate vessel, heat natural @chicle resin alongside @spruce resin or @mastic resin until completely molten and elastic. Blend a small amount of @extra virgin olive oil and liquid @sunflower lecithin into the remaining @Core or @Active syrup base, then thoroughly fold this emulsion into the hot resin matrix using a #spatula. Incorporate the pre-soaked cleaning threads uniformly into the gum base, then press and cut into uniform cubes.

*   Root-Care Core Gums: Built using the Core Hydration Base emulsion, allowing the threads to expand and rake out plaque hands-free.
*   Root-Care Active Gums: Built using the Active Base emulsion to lock cannabinoids smoothly into the gum core for systemic release during mastication.

## The Dermal Dew Line

### Background & Mission Statement
Modern bath and cleansing tools frequently rely on rigid, unyielding plastic handles and synthetic mesh poufs engineered for rapid obsolescence and the landfill. The Root-Care Bath Pad shifts this paradigm, offering an open-source, maker-accessible mechanical design that combines ergonomic structural articulation with fully removable, washable, and textile-tiered skin care. 

This architecture is specifically optimized for individuals navigating limited upper-body mobility, fine motor challenges, or sensory skin sensitivities. By utilizing a flexible, vertebrae-style wooden handle and a decoupled, multi-tier fabric sleeve system, Root-Care transforms routine bathing into a restorative, highly accessible, and low-waste ritual. Grounded in the principles of domestic stewardship and open-source material sciences, this repository provides makers with a completely customisable, non-plastic blueprint to cleanse, stimulate, and protect the skin.

### Technical Component Architecture
The handle utilizes an articulated, segmented design to provide natural flex, twist, and extended reach without relying on elastic polymers or spring metals.

Shape the @beech wood into hollowed, nested handle sleeves using a #lathe or #carving knife. Thread a double-strand line of heavy @waxed linen rope through the center of the hollowed sleeves. Tie a secure structural knot inside each sleeve chamber, nesting the knots into the internal recesses. Extend the remaining rope past the terminal sleeve at the base of the handle to form an integrated hanging loop for efficient drying.

Carve the final piece of solid @beech wood into an elongated hourglass profile from the side, creating a double-concave spoon structure with a narrow neck using #woodworking tools. Ensure the faces act as a shallow, conforming cradle to wrap flush around the natural curves of the back and limbs.

### Fabric & Fiber Texture Registries
All scrubbing and cleansing surfaces are engineered as independent, tailored fabric sleeves. Each sleeve is built with an integrated lining of @waxed cotton batting underneath the outer texturized textile, providing a soft, plush, mattress-like pad feel instead of a hard wood strike.

#### Tier 1: Cloud (The Ultra-Gentle Cleanse)
*   Utility: Optimized for sensitive skin zones, delicate mucosal areas, everyday light washing, and generating a dense, velvety lather.
*   Assembly: Layer unbleached @organic cotton flannel over the @waxed cotton batting, then cut and stitch into an hourglass pouch using a #sewing machine or #needle.

#### Tier 2: Leaf (The Gentle Exfoliant)
*   Utility: Daily full-body washing. Highly durable and naturally antimicrobial, the canvas offers a subtle, texturized grip that softens slightly in warm water.
*   Assembly: Layer woven @hemp canvas over the @waxed cotton batting, then cut and stitch into an hourglass pouch using a #sewing machine or #needle.

#### Tier 3: Bark (The True Scrub)
*   Utility: Replaces traditional, rigid loofah gourds with a flat-woven, highly breathable textile offering firm, rustic, ribbed friction for deep skin cell clearance.
*   Assembly: Layer flat-woven @jute matrix fabric over the @waxed cotton batting, then cut and stitch into an hourglass pouch using a #sewing machine or #needle.

#### Tier 4: Thorn (The Deep Revitalization)
*   Utility: Heavy, open-weave cloth that retains its prickly, uncompromising stiffness even when fully saturated. Ideal for intense wet scrubbing or dry-brushing to stimulate circulation.
*   Assembly: Layer woven @Tampico fiber cloth over the @waxed cotton batting, then cut and stitch into an hourglass pouch using a #sewing machine or #needle.

### The Hybrid Fastening System
The exchangeable sleeves are tailored as hourglass pouches that slide tightly over the wide flare of the beech head, securing firmly at the narrow neck where the handle begins. To maximize convenience without compromising on generational tool longevity, the collar utilizes a dual-action tension system.

Tailor the upper collar of the fabric sleeve pouch. Weave a continuous core of @natural latex elastic into the hem of the collar to provide a snappy stretch-on and stretch-off action. Thread a traditional @waxed linen drawstring directly alongside the latex core within the same channel. Secure the sleeve over the flared head to the narrow neck, utilizing the elastic for quick daily changes, and pull the linen drawstring taut to tie a classic slipknot when extra mechanical tension is required.

## The Ocular Dew Line

### Background & Mission Statement
Modern nocturnal eye compresses and comfort masks are heavily industrialized, almost exclusively relying on synthetic thermoplastic membranes filled with petroleum-based polyacrylate gel beads. These modern items present a distinct, wasteful lifespan; once the internal fluid volume depletes below a critical threshold or dries out, the entire synthetic assembly must be discarded, generating systemic plastic waste. Furthermore, standard household remedies fail to interface cleanly with specialized medical equipment like continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) systems. Thick, traditional cotton washcloths introduce excessive structural bulk that shifts during sleep, breaking the pneumatic seal of the CPAP frame and causing abrupt venting. Conversely, disposable paper towels lack the necessary thermal mass to sustain therapeutic cold over an extended duration.

The Root-Care Ocular Dew Line shifts this paradigm, offering an open-source, maker-accessible framework focused on targeted deep pressure therapy, total light occlusion, and absolute environmental stewardship. This architecture is specifically engineered for individuals navigating chronic dry eye, severe environmental allergies, and ocular irritation exacerbated by pressurized air micro-leaks from nocturnal respiratory masks. By utilizing a flexible, completely plant-derived hydrogel front window, a plush, light-trapping backing, and a fully refillable, bio-based thermal bead core, Root-Care transforms ocular recovery into a zero-waste, skin-safe, and medical-compatible ritual.

### Architectural Geometry (The Unified Oval)
The system utilizes a continuous, unfragmented oval silhouette that completely omits distinct ocular bulges or a nasal bridge cutout. 
* **Targeted Pressure:** The weight and displacement of the dense internal beads are focused directly over the orbital rim and eyelids—exactly where physical compression is required to stimulate the meibomian oil glands—instead of sliding down onto the cheekbones.
* **Total Blackout:** Without an exaggerated nose bump creating a tent-like gap, the backing lays entirely flush against the skin, acting as a flat gasket that absorbs photon waves to block 99.5% of ambient light.
* **CPAP Interface:** Only the ultra-thin, flat outer edges slip beneath the respiratory mask frame (such as the AirFit F30i or F20). This prevents bulky material from colliding with the cushion, ensuring the pneumatic seal remains intact while providing a physical barrier against escaping air streams.

### Technical Material Architecture
The mask body is structured as a dual-surface envelope that balances optical transparency with complete light isolation.

#### 1. The Light-Blocking Backing (Face Side)
* Utility: Rests directly against the skin, providing a plush, hypoallergenic surface that completely blocks light penetration and cushions the face from the weight of the thermal core.
* Assembly: Cut a continuous oval from heavy, high-density @cotton velvet or a @silk-cotton blend velvet. This thick, vertical pile traps all ambient light. Avoid wool to ensure the face backing remains breathable and completely non-allergenic.

#### 2. The Translucent Casing Membrane (Front Window)
* Utility: A clear, supple, and highly durable plant-born membrane that behaves like flexible synthetic vinyl, allowing the internal gel spheres to remain visible while remaining fully biodegradable.
* Assembly: Prepare a structural biopolymer backbone by mixing @sodium alginate and @LM pectin. Cross-link the network by adding @calcium glycerophosphate to form an insoluble hydrogel sheet. Incorporate @glycerin as a plant-based plasticizer to preserve flexibility at sub-zero temperatures. Add @agar to maximize transparency and structural body, and fuse a trace amount of fungal @chitosan to establish a high tensile, tear-resistant outer skin. 

#### 3. The Seamless Edge Insertion
To eliminate bulky side seams that would press painfully under CPAP headgear, the raw ends of the suspension straps are inserted directly between the front velvet layer and the clear hydrogel membrane before the final border is stitched closed, trapping the anchor completely inside the edge boundary.

### High-Endurance Suspension & Adjustment System
To eliminate petroleum-based nylon hook-and-loop (Velcro) fasteners and prevent the rapid fatigue common to raw latex bands, the tension engine mirrors the mechanical longevity of natural tissue.

#### 1. The Four-Way Stretch Strap
* Assembly: Weave raw @natural rubber threads derived from the *Hevea brasiliensis* tree directly into a dense matrix of @organic cotton yarns. The interwoven cotton acts as a built-in mechanical brake; when the cotton reaches its maximum extension, it locks to absorb pulling forces, shielding the rubber from over-stretching and preventing elastic fatigue over years of use.

#### 2. The Hybrid Plant-Burr Fastener
* The Loop Strip: Anchor a flat strip of dense, unspun @hemp felt to the suspension strap. The tangled plant fibers form a soft, breathable matrix of micro-loops.
* The Hook Strip: Weave stiffened, extra-coarse @agave fibers into micro-U-shapes and cleanly shear the tips. These rugged botanical hooks catch the hemp felt loops firmly upon mechanical contact, providing a zero-bulk, adjustable hold that won't scratch skin or snag hair.

### The Thermal Core (Spherification Matrix)
The internal filling consists of high-density, fluid gel spheres engineered to store thermal energy and conform perfectly to the contours of the eyes.

Mix @sodium alginate with a high-proportion @agar fluid base. Using a #pipette or dropper, release the liquid mixture into a cold @calcium glycerophosphate bath to initiate instant ionic spherification. The droplets will snap into perfect, bouncy, crystal-clear gel spheres. The high specific heat capacity of the agar ensures the beads retain a deep, therapeutic chill significantly longer than standard water-based compresses. Packed tightly within the mask casing, these fluid spheres distribute an even, continuous hydrostatic pressure across the eyelids to encourage natural tear-film oil expression.

### The Dynamic Biomaterial Friction Seal
The mask features a lifetime, hardware-free refill mechanism that relies on environmental physics to maintain its structural boundary.

Tailor an overlapping envelope fold along the top back boundary where the velvet meets the clear membrane. This intersection forms a smart friction and temperature seal:
* **Active State (Sealed):** When the mask is placed under physical tension (strapped to the face) or exposed to extreme temperatures (chilled in the freezer, placed in the microwave, or warmed in water), the cross-linked biopolymer chains compress and swell, locking the envelope seam completely watertight.
* **Passive State (Open):** When allowed to sit entirely relaxed at room temperature for 30 minutes without external friction, the botanical matrix relaxes and softens, allowing the envelope fold to be opened by hand so the internal agar-alginate spheres can be topped up or completely refreshed.

### Thermal Adaptability & Care Guidelines
* **Cryotherapy (Cold Use):** Store the assembled mask inside a protective linen bag in the refrigerator or freezer. The internal glycerin acts as a non-toxic antifreeze, ensuring the clear window and internal spheres remain soft, fluid, and perfectly contouring even at sub-zero temperatures.
* **Thermotherapy (Hot Use):** Place the mask in the microwave or immerse it in a warm water bath. Because agar exhibits high thermal hysteresis and the formulation is ionically cross-linked, the spheres and membrane will safely absorb and radiate comforting heat without melting back into a liquid state.
